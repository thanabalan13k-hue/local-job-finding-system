About Local Job Finding System

Making local job search easier.

Local Job Finding System is designed to connect job seekers with employment opportunities available in their local area, focusing on Tirunelveli and Tenkasi districts.

What is Local Job Finding System?

Local Job Finding System is a local employment platform that helps people discover job opportunities available near them. Instead of searching through many different sources, users can search jobs based on job title, district, job type, qualification and skills.

The platform also provides a resume-based job matching feature. Users can provide their resume, qualification and skills to discover suitable job opportunities available in Tirunelveli and Tenkasi.
